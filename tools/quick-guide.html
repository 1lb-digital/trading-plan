Six ground rules

  1. No real money until Gate 5 is clear.
  2. All practice in a paper account.
  3. Every practice trade goes in the log.
  4. Personal account, not business. Ask the tax preparer first.
  5. Never add to a losing trade.
  6. Risk 1% per trade, maximum ($10 on $1,000).

R, the number to track

R is what you risked. Risk $10 and make $20: +2R. Lose the $10: -1R. Expectancy is the average R per trade; positive means the strategy makes money over many trades.

Go or no go: fund $1,000 only if all five hold

  1. 40 or more paper trades logged
  2. Total R positive across the sample
  3. Rule adherence 90% or higher
  4. No loss larger than the planned maximum
  5. The one pager never changed during the sample

Account rules, 2026. FINRA replaced the $25,000 pattern day trader rule with intraday margin requirements on June 4, 2026. Brokers may keep the old rule until October 20, 2027, so ask yours. Cash stock accounts and micro futures have no day trade limit. CFDs are not legal for US retail traders.

Scam checklist: close anything that

promises guaranteed or passive returns · shows backtests but no verified live record · earns its money selling the bot or course · says "AI" without explaining the model · sells a prop firm challenge or uses countdown timers · shows a results chart with no losing stretches.

Build the YouTube-trained agent

Phase 6 and "Try it with Claude"
1lb-digital.github.io/trading-plan

An AI agent reads one trader's videos, writes exact rules, backtests them and runs them in a paper account. The language model works before the market opens; tested code places the orders. Most video strategies fail the backtest, and the gates catch them before they cost money.

The build in six steps

1 HarvestPick one trader who states exact rules and shows losses. Pull transcripts with youtube-transcript-api (free, run it on your own computer) or yt-dlp --write-auto-subs --skip-download. NotebookLM cross-checks rules across many videos.
2 ExtractAI writes strategy.yaml: instrument, timeframe, session, signals, entry, stop, target, 1% position size, daily loss limit. Every rule quotes the transcript; vague points go under "undefined" until you decide them.
3 Backtestbacktesting.py on Alpaca's free bars (7+ years) or yfinance (intraday only 60 days). Hold back the last 30% of the data until the rules are final.
4 PaperA bot on Alpaca's free paper account sends bracket orders (entry with stop and target), enforces the loss limit and size cap, logs every decision, and has a kill switch.
5 GateBacktest: 30+ out of sample trades, expectancy above 0R, drawdown under 10R. Paper: 40+ trades, expectancy above 0R, no loss over 1R plus slippage, rules followed on every trade.
6 Small liveOnly after Gate 5 and go or no go. 1% risk. Back to paper if drawdown reaches 10R or expectancy is at or below 0R after 40 trades.

Try it with Claude: setup

  1. Get Claude Pro ($20 a month; Claude Code is not in the free plan) at claude.com/pricing.
  2. Install the Claude desktop app from claude.com/download (macOS 13+ or Windows 10 1809+) and sign in.
  3. Create an empty folder Documents/trading-agent.
  4. Download CLAUDE.md from the plan's "Try it with Claude" section into that folder.
  5. In the app, click "Code", choose a local environment, select the folder, and set permission mode to "Manual".
  6. Paste the start prompt and press Enter. Approve each step, then type "next".
  7. Open a free Alpaca account, generate paper API keys, and paste them into the .env file yourself. Never paste keys into chat.
